# Trust, scope and limitations

> [!WARNING]
> Sanitised public showcase. Not for clinical use as-is, not legal or regulatory advice, and not a product. Last verified 21 Oct 2026.

## What this repository is
Case study and playbook (documentation). Engineers and founders who run agent fleets and want to cut token spend with measured, repeatable methods.

## Out of scope
It is not a pricing guarantee, a benchmark of model providers or a hosted tool.

## Limitations
- Savings were measured on the author's fleets; your baseline and workload decide your result.
- Rupee figures depend on provider prices at the time of measurement.
- 18 of the author's cost methods are published here; the rest stay private.

## Data provenance
No prompts, configurations, client data or credentials. Figures come from the author's token telemetry; the brokerage client is anonymised. Every release passes five gates before it is public: G1 allow-list of files, G2 secret and deny-list scan, G3 fresh history (no private commits travel), G4 fresh-context AI review (an agent that did not write the material), G5 owner approval (the human check) with a licence check. See the [publication policy](https://github.com/sterilefield/.github/blob/main/PUBLICATION_POLICY.md).

## Privacy
No personal data is processed by anything in this repository.
